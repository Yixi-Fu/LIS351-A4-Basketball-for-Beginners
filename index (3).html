Three skills to build first

A simple starting point for new players: control the ball, build a repeatable shot, and learn how to move on defense.

1

Dribbling Basics

Keep the ball low, practice with both hands, and learn to look up instead of watching the ball.

2

Shooting Fundamentals

Start close to the basket and focus on balance, hand placement, and a relaxed follow-through.

3

Defense & Movement

Stay balanced, keep your feet active, and practice sliding without crossing your feet.

Your First Basketball Practice

A short routine is enough to build confidence. Repeat these steps consistently before adding more advanced drills.

Simple Practice Routine

  1. Warm up and stretch for five to ten minutes.
  2. Practice low, controlled dribbles with both hands.
  3. Take close-range shots and focus on your form.
  4. Practice defensive slides without crossing your feet.
  5. Finish with ten free throws and record how many you make.

Basketball Gear

Beginners do not need expensive equipment. A basketball, athletic clothing, supportive shoes, and access to a court are enough to get started.

Court Etiquette

Respect other players, share the court, call fouls fairly, communicate clearly, and remember that basketball depends on teamwork.
